## Opis

```
Works right away — open the file, enter the job size, get the price. No Canva account, no links, no rewriting.

A real spreadsheet (Excel + Google Sheets) for pressure washing and gutter cleaning businesses. Type the square footage and linear feet, pick LOW / MID / HIGH pricing, and you get:
• the quote price (driveway, house wash, roof soft wash, gutters)
• your real cost: crew hours, driving, chemicals
• your profit margin
• a clear verdict: OK – take the job / LOW – quote $X instead / REJECT – walk away
• a ready-to-send quote message for your customer

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT
Most templates are a Canva link you have to fill in by hand. This one does the math. Every default rate shows its 2026 source, and you can change any rate to match your area.

WHAT YOU GET
• 1 XLSX file (4 tabs: START, RATES, CALC, COPY)
• 1 PDF: how to open it in Google Sheets on phone or computer

HOW IT WORKS
1. Download and open in Excel or Google Sheets (free).
2. Fill the yellow cells on START.
3. Copy the quote text from the COPY tab and send it.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Digital download — nothing is shipped.
• Prices are in USD; rates are national ranges, adjust them to your market.
• This product was designed with the help of AI tools and checked and tested by me by hand (formulas verified on 4 job scenarios).
• Questions? Message me — I answer within 24 h.
```
