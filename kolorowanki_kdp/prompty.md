# Pakiet promptów do kolorowanek (LitPal → Amazon KDP)

Każdy zestaw: pola wklejasz **osobno** (patrz `JAK_UZYWAC.md`). Prompty po angielsku —
generator lepiej rozumie EN, a rynek KDP.com jest największy.

Wspólne zasady wbudowane w każdy Description: czarne kontury, czysta biel, brak
cieniowania, jeden główny motyw na stronę, nic nie ucięte przy krawędzi, brak tekstu.

---

## 1. Large Print — dorośli 60+ / słaby wzrok

**Title**
```
Large Print Coloring Book for Seniors: Simple Flowers, Birds and Country Scenes
```
**Description**
```
A relaxing coloring book for seniors and adults with low vision or arthritis. Each page shows ONE simple, calm subject from everyday life: a flower pot, a songbird on a branch, a teacup with roses, a barn with a fence, a butterfly, a lighthouse. Shapes are large and open, with wide spaces that are easy to color with thick markers or crayons. Very bold, uniform black outlines (about 3-4 mm), pure white background, generous margins on all sides. Few details, no tiny patterns.
Avoid: shading, gray tones, hatching, filled black areas, small intricate details, text or letters, busy backgrounds, anything cut off at the page edge.
```
**Art Style**
```
Bold clean line art, thick even outlines, simple flat shapes, black and white, no shading
```
**Pages:** test 4 → 40 · **Trim:** 8.5 × 11 · **Quality:** Standard

```
An alphabet coloring book for toddlers ages 2-5. Each page shows ONE large friendly animal whose name starts with the letter of the page. Very thick outlines, very simple shapes, pure white background, no shading, no extra objects.
```

## Szybka naprawa typowych błędów

| Problem na stronie testowej | Dopisz do Description |
|---|---|
| szare cieniowanie | `Strictly pure black lines on pure white. Zero gray, zero shading.` |
| czarne wypełnione plamy | `No solid black fills anywhere; every area must be white and colorable.` |
| ucięty motyw | `The entire subject fits inside the page with a wide white margin on all sides.` |
| za dużo drobiazgów | `Fewer, larger shapes. Minimum detail. Leave big empty areas to color.` |
| przypadkowe litery | `Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, signs or logos.` |
